Hello, hello! I’m Coach Dee.

A coach, educator, believer, and fellow work in progress.

Project Vitamin Dee was born from a simple desire: to help people feel seen, heard, encouraged, and hopeful.

I don’t believe coaching is about giving you a perfect formula or telling you how to live your life. It’s about creating space for honest reflection, helping you recognize what may be keeping you stuck, and walking with you as you discover your next faithful step.

You may arrive carrying questions, exhaustion, heartbreak, or uncertainty. My prayer is that you leave with greater clarity, courage, peace, and hope.

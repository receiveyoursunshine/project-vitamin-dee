Hello, hello! I’m Coach Dee.

I know what it feels like to smile while carrying silent battles.

To look strong while secretly feeling tired. To believe in God while still asking difficult questions. To keep showing up while quietly wondering whether healing will ever come.

Project Vitamin Dee was born because I discovered that healing does not always happen through one dramatic moment.

Sometimes, healing begins with one honest conversation.

I’m a coach, educator, pastor, speaker, and fellow work in progress. I’m not here because I have life perfectly figured out. I’m here because I have experienced what hope can do.

Now, I simply pass that sunshine forward.
